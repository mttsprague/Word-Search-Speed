Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?
Simply find all the words in the puzzle grid as quickly as possible. Swipe to select words horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
What are the different difficulty levels?
The game offers Easy, Medium, and Hard difficulty levels with varying grid sizes and word complexity.
How does Game Center work?
Sign in with Game Center to compete on leaderboards and track your best times across all difficulty levels.
How can I improve my speed?
Practice regularly, start with easier levels, and try to spot word patterns quickly. The more you play, the faster you'll get!

Privacy
