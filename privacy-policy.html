Privacy Policy

Word Search Speed

Last Updated: December 23, 2025

Introduction

Welcome to Word Search Speed. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

In Short: Word Search Speed collects minimal data to provide you with the best gaming experience. We use third-party advertising (Google AdMob) to support the free version of the app, but we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Information We Collect

Automatically Collected Information

When you use Word Search Speed, we may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Game Statistics: Your puzzle completion times, scores, and difficulty levels played (stored locally on your device)
  • Game Center Data: If you choose to use Game Center, your leaderboard scores and achievements are shared through Apple's Game Center service
  • Device Information: Basic device information such as device type, operating system version, and app version for troubleshooting purposes

Advertising Information

Word Search Speed displays advertisements through Google AdMob to support the free version of the app. We do not track you across other apps or websites. The ads you see are non-personalized and based on contextual information rather than your personal data or browsing history.

AdMob may collect certain limited information to serve ads and prevent fraud, including:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and app version
  • Ad Interaction Data: Whether you view or tap an ad (not linked to your identity)
  • General Location: Country and region (derived from IP address, not precise location)

No Cross-App Tracking: We do not use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to track you across other apps or websites. The ads you see are not based on your personal information or activity outside of Word Search Speed.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain the Word Search Speed gaming experience
  • Track your personal best times and statistics (stored locally)
  • Enable Game Center features like leaderboards and achievements
  • Display relevant advertisements to support the free version of the app
  • Improve and optimize app performance
  • Troubleshoot issues and provide customer support

Data Sharing and Third Parties

Apple Game Center

If you choose to use Game Center features, your scores and achievements are shared through Apple's Game Center service. This data is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Google AdMob to display non-personalized ads in the app. AdMob collects limited, non-identifying information to serve contextual ads and ensure ad quality. This data is not used to track you or build a profile about you across other apps or websites.

For more information about how Google AdMob handles data, please visit Google's Privacy Policy

  • Use information from other apps and websites you visit

    • Our advertising partners include companies that comply with industry standards and self-regulatory frameworks.

    Data Storage and Security

    Your game statistics and preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not collect or store your personal game data on external servers. We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

    Children's Privacy

    Word Search Speed is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app does not require personal information to play, and all tracking is optional and controlled by iOS parental controls.

    Your Privacy Rights and Choices

    You have the following rights regarding your information:

      Delete App Data: You can delete all locally stored game data by uninstalling the app from your device
    • Reset Statistics: You can reset your game statistics within the app settings at any time
    • Game Center Control: You can manage your Game Center data through your Apple ID settings
    • Ad Preferences: While we don't use personalized ads, you can manage general iOS ad settings through Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertisingettings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising > Reset Advertising Identifier
    • Delete App Data: You can delete all app data by uninstalling the app from your device

    Data Retention

    Since game data is stored locally on your device, it remains there until you delete the app or manually reset your statistics within the app. We do not retain any personal data on external servers.

    Changes to This Privacy Policy

    We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

    International Users

    Word Search Speed is available worldwide. If you are using the app from outside the United States, please be aware that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate.

    California Privacy Rights

    If you are a California resident, you have specific rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Since we do not sell personal information and collect minimal data, these rights primarily relate to your ability to opt-out of tracking as described above.

    GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

    If you are in the European Union, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including:

    • Right to access your personal data
    • Right to rectification of inaccurate data
    • Right to erasure of your data
    • Right to restrict processing
    • Right to data portability
    • Right to object to processing

    Since all game data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over this data through your device settings.
